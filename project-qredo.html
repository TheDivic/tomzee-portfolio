Qredo

Scaling to Entrprise-Grade Crypto Trading

Web App, 2021-2024

Who are Qredo?

Enterprise-focused digital assed deployment and storage solution that uses the MPC (Multi-Party Computation) technology with the goal of removing the need of a user painpoint which are private keys. The result? Utilizing Qredo, the large institutions and smaller firms can seamlessly collaborate across multiple investment wallets and funds, never before seen in the crypto/blockchain space.

Visit Qredo

My role and responsibilities

General UX design and content design

I joined Qredo as a UX writer and this is where I upskilled to a product designer. Never before have I had such an impact on a product, where I learned how important the UX content for the overall design on the platform is. While I performed the typical UX design tasks, including component creation, responsive design solutions, design system management, I was the sole designer responsible of the content on the entire platform. This included designing, deploying, and curating the entire Qredo’s Help Center.

Opportunities and goals of the project

Improving how the platform looks and feels, while deploying the structural changes of the entire product.

I came in when Qredo already had the initial wireframes, which detailed a very basic structure and flows for an MVP. The product design team I was on was in the process of creating the MVP, but simultaneously making visual and functional updates to the product.

Biggest challenge faced

Our team leader, Will, spearheaded a very bold effort of working on the visual and structural updates of the platform, while keeping them under wraps. All the while, we also worked on the needs of the current MVP state of the product. This was a very challenging time that required a lot of after-hour time, but it all paid off. When the preview of the new structure and feel of Qredo was presented to the CEO, he immediately green-lit the project.

Outcome and learnings

Over the course of the next few months, the product design team worked tirelessly to deliver the new era of Qredo. We called the project “Purple Rain” (yes, as a Prince reference, but mostly because the new brand color was selected as purple). The biggest career benefit I got from Qredo was that I worked on the best Product Design team I’ve ever had an honor to work with thus far. However, I’d be amiss if I didn’t mention the challenges and learning opportunities I had as someone working closely with FE engineers and Project Managers, but also with blockchain engineers, cryptographers, and various stakeholders.
