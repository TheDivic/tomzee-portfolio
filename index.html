I’m a Product Designer with a background in Web, UX, UI, and Content Design. Over the years, I’ve worked across a range of industries and products—from fintech and web3 to research platforms, SaaS, and consumer applications.

I enjoy getting stuck into complex problems, understanding what people actually need, and turning that understanding into products that feel clear, intuitive, and genuinely useful.

What I do.

I work across the full product design process, from discovery and defining problems through to UX, UI, prototyping, design systems, and developer handoff.

My background in UX writing also gives me a particular focus on the words within an interface. I believe good products don’t just look good—they communicate clearly, guide users confidently, and make complicated things feel simple.

I’m comfortable working on everything from early concepts and 0→1 products to established platforms that need refinement, structure, and a stronger user experience.

How I like to work.

I believe the best design comes from understanding the problem before jumping into solutions. I’m used to collaborating closely with product managers, developers, researchers, and other designers, using discussion, iteration, and feedback to challenge assumptions and arrive at better solutions. I’m a firm believer of finding the right solution to a problem—attaching oneself to the early idea is a slippery slope.

I’m also a big believer in balancing user needs with business and technical realities. Great product design sits somewhere in the middle: it should solve a real problem for the user while creating meaningful value for the product and the business.

Whether I’m working independently or as part of a larger team, I value clear communication, thoughtful decision-making, and a healthy amount of curiosity.

Where I’ve worked.

Throughout my career, I’ve had the opportunity to work with startups, established companies, and teams across a variety of industries, including fintech, web3, SaaS, research, and other digital products.

Some of my work has involved designing complex dashboards and platforms, building new product experiences from the ground up, creating design systems, improving existing user journeys, and shaping the content and interaction patterns that bring products to life.

I’m always interested in the next interesting problem—especially when it’s complex enough to require more than just making things look good.
