Hanover Research

High-quality research & analytics

Web App, 2021-2024

Who are Hanover Research?

An enterprise research and insights platform that gives organizations access to expert research, data, and actionable insights. The platform helps teams explore industry trends, benchmark performance, access research reports, and turn complex information into clearer, more confident decisions.

My role and responsibilities

I performed tasks of a permanent and main Product Designer on the project.

Our product design and development agency was tasked with creating a research and insights dashboard that the clients would use as a UX-friendly access point for getting access to a variety of content that the Hanover Research offers.

Opportunities and goals of the project

Making a dashboard that is user-friendly and easy to use.

As an already established company, Hanover Research contacted our design and development agency to help boost their users’ access to a variety of research material the company offers. As such, during the discovery, it was decided that a comprehensive dashboard be created. This involved communicating back-and-forth with the client and ensuring their company needs are covered. Naturally, this was all done with the help of a project manager and a team of developers, with whom the design team collaborated tirelessly.

Timeline illustrationsassets/img/hanover-rail-2.png

Biggest challenge faced

This project involved setting up a detailed design system, to cater to comprehensive responsive measurements. This included non-traditional sizes such as phone portrait, phone landscape, tablet portrait, tablet landscape, desktop (1440px), as well as desktop (1920px) sizes. Although this proved to be quite a challenge at certain points, I am happy to say that we’ve managed to tackle the demanding responsive screen types for Hanover Research.

Outcome and learnings

I’m especially proud of how the new user tour (which I spearheaded) came out; even though we had a lot of information to display, the tour was effective in making everything appear clean, simple, and easy to use, thereby ensuring user retention. This part of the app was met with positive feedback.

Ultimately, the project was completed successfully and to complete client’s satisfaction, which is something I wear as a badge of honor.
